About NEXUS AI

The story behind the intelligence

🚀 What We Built

NEXUS AI combines cutting-edge 3D visuals with real artificial intelligence to create an interactive experience unlike a typical website. Every question you ask is answered by a real AI model, wrapped in a beautiful animated interface.

🎨 The Technology

Built with Three.js for stunning WebGL 3D graphics, Node.js + Express for the backend, and powered by advanced language models to deliver real, intelligent answers — not pre-programmed responses.

💡 The Vision

We believe the future of web interfaces is conversational and visual. NEXUS AI is a glimpse into that future — where you don't browse a website, you talk to it.
